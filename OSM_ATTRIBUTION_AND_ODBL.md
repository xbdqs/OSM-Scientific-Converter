# OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL

The software does not include the real validation PBF files. Users supply their own OSM snapshot and remain responsible for its license obligations.

OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL). When publishing maps, databases, extracts, or analyses derived from OSM, provide visible attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors and review whether the output is a Derivative Database, a Produced Work, or otherwise subject to ODbL share-alike or notice requirements.

Suggested short attribution: `© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0`.

The converter preserves OSM identifiers, original tags in `tags_json`, the input SHA-256, and profile/configuration hashes to support provenance. These technical records do not replace legal attribution. The synthetic `examples/sample_infrastructure.osm` file was created for this software and is not extracted from the OpenStreetMap database.

Authoritative terms should be checked at the OpenStreetMap Foundation copyright and ODbL pages before public redistribution. This file is technical guidance, not legal advice.
